---
title: DatePicker
description: 日期与日期范围选择，支持多选、确认及独立替换触发元素、面板、容器和底部。
---

## 何时使用

需要选择日期或日期范围时使用，例如预约日期、活动起止日期和按日期筛选数据。

```text
DatePickerRoot
├── DatePickerTrigger (Input / custom trigger)
└── DatePickerContent (Popover / custom container)
    ├── DatePickerPanel (Calendar / custom panel)
    └── DatePickerFooter (optional)
        ├── DatePickerToday
        ├── DatePickerCancel (needConfirm)
        └── DatePickerConfirm (needConfirm)

RangePickerRoot
├── RangePickerTrigger (start input + end input)
└── RangePickerContent
    ├── RangePickerPanelGroup
    │   ├── RangePickerPanel
    │   └── RangePickerPanel
    └── DatePickerFooter (optional)
```
